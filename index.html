Who I Am

Hi, I'm DJS (Debasis/Jaga/Sebasi). I'm a freelancer and the owner of MineCloud, where I provide services like website development, Discord server setup, Minecraft texture pack design, and plugin development.

My Passion

I've always been passionate about creativity and building things that bring people together. Whether it's designing websites, improving a community server, or working on game-related projects, I enjoy finding smart and simple ways to make ideas come to life.

Gaming & Development

I'm also a gamer at heart I spend most of my free time in Minecraft and Roblox, which has inspired me to keep learning more about development and online communities. Through MineCloud, I've had the chance to collaborate with different people, grow my skills, and help others build their projects.

My Approach

I believe in taking things step by step, focusing on quality, and always keeping a positive, friendly approach. Every project is an opportunity to create something meaningful and help bring someone's vision to life.
